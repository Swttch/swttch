## If your problem is not here

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Swttch/swttch/issues/new/choose) and tell us about it.

Including your IDE and its version, the plugin version, and your operating system helps a lot in finding the cause.

If you are on Linux, please also tell us your desktop environment (KDE, GNOME, and so on) and your session type (Wayland or X11).

You can browse what has already been reported in the [issue list](https://github.com/Swttch/swttch/issues).
